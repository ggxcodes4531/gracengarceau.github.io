“Stay Locked In” Phone Reminder

A motion-triggered reminder prototype designed to help me remember my phone before leaving my dorm.

ECE 1100 · Fall 2026

Project overview

After getting locked out of my dorm several times because I left my phone inside, I wanted a reminder that would catch my attention as I headed out. “Stay Locked In” is a door-mounted prototype that uses motion to trigger an audible alert and a message on an LCD.

01 · Define

The Problem

Leaving my phone inside meant I could get locked out of my dorm. After it happened several times, I wanted a simple reminder that would activate as I approached the door. The goal was to make the reminder noticeable without relying on another person to prompt me.

02 · Develop

The Process

I built the prototype around an Arduino Mega, a motion sensor, a buzzer, and an LCD display. The sensor is intended to detect movement within roughly two feet. When it detects someone leaving, the buzzer sounds and the display flashes “DON’T FORGET YOUR PHONE, GRACEN!” The components are powered by a 5-volt battery pack, and the setup is designed to mount by the door.

I used C++ in the Arduino IDE. To reduce wiring mistakes and avoid risking components, I checked the initial board wiring and added components incrementally, testing their integration as I went.

03 · Deliver

The Outcome & Next Steps

The result is a prototype that combines a motion trigger with sound and an on-screen reminder. Building it in stages helped me check the hardware as I added each part. A useful next step would be to test the sensor’s behavior in the doorway, refine when the reminder activates, and evaluate how reliably the alert is noticed in everyday use.
