Project overview

The problem I set out to solve with the project was getting locked out of my dorm. Over five times early on in the semester I was stuck outside due to leaving my phone inside my dorm. I wanted to find a soultion that would force me to remeber to have my phone without being prompted by my roommate. I used an Arduindo Mega board, a motion sensor, a buzzar, and and LCD display to create a device which would grab my attention and check to see if I had my phone with me. The Aurduino Mega Board was connected to teh motion sensor and was rated to detect movement within roughly 2 feet. This then triggers a high pitch buzzar noise to alert me followed by a message on the LCD display blinking "DON'T FORGET YOUR PHONE GRACEN!" The set up is powered by a 5 volt battery pack and allows the set up to mounted directly onto the door.
